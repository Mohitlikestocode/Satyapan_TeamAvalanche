How Satyapan works

Satyapan does not say "fake" or "real". It shows you what it found, where the information came from, and what it could not check, so you can decide.

1

Upload

Screenshot or photo
PDF invoice
Message, link, QR, voice

No login. You agree to what will be checked first.

2

We check

Signs a screenshot or PDF was edited
Who is asking for the money
What the words and the voice say

Each check is ticked only when it really runs. Your file is deleted after.

3

You decide

Verdict in words, with reasons
Ask family · Listen · Report
Next steps you take yourself

Satyapan never pays, blocks, accuses or files anything for you.

What each check can and cannot do

Every row below is something that really runs in this site today. Where something is a limit, it says so.

Test results

Every number is measured and published on the Results page, with the test set, the interval, the false alarms and the named failures. Anything we did not measure says "not evaluated". We do not show estimates.

See the measured results Attack it yourself

Known failure cases

Scams written like ordinary messages. Edited screenshots that leave few pixel traces. Real phone screenshots: not evaluated. Cloned voices over phone lines. Real businesses that genuinely changed banks.
