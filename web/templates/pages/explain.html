Showing the same findings in everyday words.

The bank code points to a different bank

Every bank branch has a code. The code on this bill belongs to a different bank than the name written on it. Someone may have changed the bank details.

Technical: IFSC mismatch (HDFC0001234 ≠ ICICI Bank)
2

The bill was changed after it was made

The bank part of the bill was typed in later, in a different style of letters from the rest.

Technical: PDF modified after creation, font mismatch on bank lines
3

Last time they asked you to pay a different bank

Their earlier bill had another bank. Companies sometimes change banks, but scammers also use this trick.

Technical: payee bank differs from previous invoice

In short: call the seller on a number you already have before you send ₹1,48,500.
