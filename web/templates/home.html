Scam patterns to watch for Samples

Investment
"Guaranteed 30% a month" stock-tips group

A cloned "expert" in an ad, a VIP group, then a QR that pays a personal UPI ID. Registered brokers collect only on @valid handles.

See the sample result
Business
"Our bank account has changed"

An invoice shows new bank details while the IFSC code points to a different bank. Confirm on a phone number you already have.

See the sample result
Shops
A "payment successful" screenshot

The amount or reference number has been edited. A screenshot is not proof of payment: check your own UPI app or soundbox.

See the sample result
Calls
"Amazon refund pending, install support app"

Official companies do not ask you to install apps or share a screen. Use their own app or website instead.

Common questions

Will Satyapan tell me something is "safe"?

No. The best answer is "no red flags found", and it always says what was and was not checked. Nobody can promise a payment is safe.

What happens to my file?

It is deleted after checking. We keep only the result, a report ID and a file fingerprint. Voice goes to a speech service only if you agree.

Does Satyapan block or report anything for me?

No. It is advice only. If you want to report, we prepare a draft and you file it yourself on cybercrime.gov.in or by calling 1930.

What if the result is wrong?

Use "Wrong result?" on any result. The case goes to a human reviewer, and you can see its status.

Which languages does it support?

English and Hindi, with a spoken version of the short verdict. More Indian languages are planned.
