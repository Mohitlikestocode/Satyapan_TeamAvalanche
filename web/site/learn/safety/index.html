Six rules that stop most scams

Print this or keep it near your phone.

1

Never trust a screenshot as proof of payment

Check your own app, soundbox or bank statement.

2

Verify bank-detail changes on a number you already have

Never the number in the email or invoice.

3

Never share OTP, PIN or passwords

No bank, company or Satyapan will ask for them.

4

Check advisers on SEBI's website

Type the address yourself. Registered brokers use @valid UPI IDs.

5

Real bank websites end in .bank.in

Look closely: "amazon-indiasale.com" is not amazon.in.

6

If you've paid, call 1930 and your bank

The sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.
